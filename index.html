Questions a checkup answers

Am I actually making money?

Your monthly profit after product costs, rent, and bills, and how it compares to what you want to pay yourself.

How many sales do I need?

Your break-even point, per month and per day, and how far sales could drop before you lose money.

Can I raise my prices?

If you raise prices 10%, how many customers you could lose and still earn the same as today.

Which items earn the money?

Every product's real margin, so you can find the best-sellers that quietly lose money.

Will cash last?

A 13-week forecast that shows whether a tax bill or repair will push your bank balance below zero.

Did the changes help?

A free 30-day follow-up that compares your numbers before and after.

How it works

  1. 1

    Request a time

    Pick a slow half hour. We come to you, and rough numbers are fine. Most owners know them within about 10%.

  2. 2

    Work through it together

    We enter your prices, costs, sales, and bills, then go over the results with you in plain language.

  3. 3

    Leave with one next step

    You get a one-page report to print or save, plus one specific change worth trying first.

  4. 4

    Check in after 30 days

    A short follow-up to see what changed and what to try next.

Your numbers stay yours

The checkup tool runs entirely in the web browser. There is no account, no server, and no tracking. Your numbers are never uploaded anywhere. We only keep a copy if you say yes, and any patterns we share across many businesses never include names.

What this is and isn't

This is a free educational checkup run by a student volunteer. It isn't accounting, tax, or legal advice. For those, we'll point you to free help like your local Small Business Development Center (SBDC).

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no fee, no upsell, and nothing to sign up for.

Why are you doing this?

Most small businesses can't afford a financial advisor, and many owners have never seen their break-even point. This project is a way to learn business finance by doing it with real owners, while giving them something useful.

What do I need to bring?

Rough monthly numbers: your average sale, what it costs you to make or deliver, how many sales you make, your fixed bills (rent, utilities, insurance), and your bank balance. If you have prices and costs for your main products, bring those too.

My books are a mess. Can I still do it?

Yes, that's common. Estimates are enough to see the big picture, and the checkup often shows which numbers are worth tracking more carefully.

Who sees my numbers?

Only you and the person running the checkup, on one screen. Nothing is uploaded. You can take the saved file with you.

Can I use the tool on my own?

Yes. Open the checkup tool and click “Load sample café” to see how it works. It also works offline once loaded.
